Most of the conventional wisdom around rewrite vs all the alternatives came down to simple economics: the time to rewrite an entire application was more expensive than the alternatives: refactoring, optimizing, or even just running the application on a more powerful server. 

But most of that conventional wisdom came around before LLM-coding.

By now, most of us know that [Bun](https://bun.com) was rewritten in [Rust](https://rust-lang.org.), [mostly by AI](https://bun.com/blog/bun-in-rust). While [the estimated $165,000 worth of tokens](https://bun.com/blog/bun-in-rust#stats) it took to rewrite sounds staggering, it's comparable to [some American developer's (median) yearly salary](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/software-developers.htm#tab-5). As of this writing, there's already [significant payoff from it](https://bun.com/blog/bun-in-rust#bun-is-better-in-rust).
